Hi, I’m Moena.

Through running my own online English school and publishing a Kindle book—which achieved the #1 spot in 14 categories—I have practical, hands-on experience in web marketing focused on turning ideas into reality and effectively reaching target audiences.

Leveraging this experience, I provide web design services using Figma and STUDIO, alongside social media marketing support, all tailored closely to each client’s unique challenges.

This website showcases my design portfolio and my entrepreneurial journey.
